# What is ClarityForge?

ClarityForge is a **computer-side** research desk: a paper-trading companion that treats cognitive state as a first-class safety input. It is not a broker, not a decoder, and not a medical product.

It sits next to NeuraGuard (state firewall) and NeuraShell (control plane). Guard watches intention streams for fatigue and privacy. ClarityForge uses the same family of mock states to **slow down trading intent** when Decision Quality is not High.

## Why it exists

Most trading UIs optimize for speed. ClarityForge optimizes for **cognitive hygiene**: a visible state panel, a single quality status, and extra confirmation when you are tired, over-aroused, or unlike your own better paper history.

## What it is not

- Not implant firmware
- Not a medical device
- Not a Neuralink product
- Not a live brokerage
- Not financial advice
